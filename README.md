# CloudTV 4.1 for macOS

# [CloudTV for macOS](https://technicalworld.co/cloudtv/)

CloudTV offers access to over 400 live TV channels from around the globe, including more than 100 English-speaking channels. It allows you to stream or permanently download thousands of catch-up programs from BBC channels (requiring a UK IP address) and a curated selection of French channels. With smart playlists, you can customize and build your ideal TV lineup by filtering catch-up programs according to your personal preferences.
